Acorns Nest Inc. ("Acorns Nest", "we", "us", "our") provides recordkeeping and communication software licensed to childcare centres. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in the Acorns Nest mobile app, the director dashboard, and this website (together, the "Service").

This policy describes our practices. Consent for a child's information to be recorded in the Service is obtained by your childcare centre as described in section 10.

## 1. Our role and your childcare centre's role

Acorns Nest acts as a service provider to childcare centres. Your centre owns and controls the information it records in the Service. Your centre decides what information is recorded about your child, who at the centre may view it, and how long it is kept. Your centre obtains consent from families at enrolment and remains accountable for that information under applicable privacy legislation.

We process personal information on your centre's instructions and for the purpose of delivering, supporting, and securing the Service. We do not use it for our own purposes.

Requests to view, correct, or delete a child's information should be directed to the centre's director in the first instance. We will also act on requests made directly to us and will coordinate with your centre where necessary.

For visitors to this website, and for the operation and security of the Service itself, Acorns Nest is the responsible organization.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information

Names, email addresses, optional phone numbers and profile photographs, assigned role, and centre membership for parents, guardians, teachers, kitchen staff, and directors. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider and cannot be read by us or by your centre.

### 2.2 Information about children

Names, classroom assignment, date of birth, profile photograph, and guardian relationships, entered by centre staff at enrolment.

### 2.3 Health and safety information

Allergies, dietary requirements, medications, physician contact details, care notes, emergency contacts, and authorized pickup persons.

### 2.4 Daily care records

Attendance, sleep, meal and bottle records, absence requests, and outing details, recorded by staff during the day and made available to parents.

### 2.5 Photographs and messages

Classroom photographs taken or uploaded by staff, together with captions and the intended audience. Announcements and messages written by staff and directors, which may reference a child by name.

### 2.6 Staff employment information

Role classification, compensation rate, and submitted working hours.

### 2.7 Technical and centre information

Device notification tokens, device platform and app version, and session information required to keep you signed in. For each centre, we hold the centre name, licence number, time zone, and operational settings configured by the director.

### 2.8 Payments

If a centre turns on online payments, families pay through Stripe. Card and bank details are entered directly with Stripe and held by Stripe. We receive payment status and amounts so the centre can see what has been paid.

### 2.9 This website

If you accept analytics on this website, we record the pages you view, how you arrived, and whether the walkthrough form was submitted. We do not record your name, email address, phone number, or the other answers on that form for this purpose. If you decline, or have not yet chosen, we do not collect this information. Section 7 explains the choice and the cookies.

## 3. Information we do not collect

Acorns Nest does not collect:

- Home or residential addresses
- Payment card or bank account numbers (these are held by Stripe, as described in section 2.8)
- Government-issued identifiers, including Social Insurance Numbers and health card numbers
- Device location
- Audio recordings
- Contacts, calendar, or address book information
- Advertising identifiers

We do not operate advertising on the Service, and we do not track users across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |

## 5. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- Maintain the daily childcare records your centre is required to keep
- Make allergy, medication, and emergency contact information available to supervising staff
- Provide parents with information about their child's day
- Deliver announcements, alerts, and photographs to the intended recipients
- Generate attendance reporting and process staff timesheets
- Process payments when a centre turns on online payments
- Authenticate users, secure accounts, and prevent misuse
- Provide the artificial intelligence features described in section 6.3
- Understand how visitors find and use this website, only when they have agreed, as described in section 7

We do not use children's information for marketing or profiling, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

## 6. How we share information

### 6.1 Within your childcare centre

Access is limited by role. A parent may view only the records of their own children, and only those announcements and approved photographs addressed to them. A teacher may view only children in classrooms to which that teacher is assigned. A director may view records of that director's centre only.

### 6.2 Access by Acorns Nest

Only the founders of Acorns Nest can access a centre's records, and only when it is needed: to give support a director has asked for, to fix a problem, to keep the Service secure, or where the law requires it. Every access is logged. We will share those access logs with a centre when it is necessary, or if a breach or other incident is suspected.

### 6.3 Artificial intelligence features

Some features use artificial intelligence through Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini). These features include reading uploaded menu documents into the meal planner, nutrition summaries for parents, and Ask Acorn, a read-only question tool for directors where a centre has it turned on.

To answer a director's question, Ask Acorn sends the relevant records from that director's own centre, which can include children's names and attendance and care entries. Our Vertex AI setup uses zero data retention: Google does not store the requests or the answers after responding, and they are not used to train any AI model.

Output generated by these features is informational only. It may be inaccurate or incomplete, and it does not replace staff judgment. It does not constitute medical, nutritional, or developmental advice. Ask Acorn must not be used for headcounts or in an emergency.

We engage a small number of providers to deliver the Service. Each is bound by contract to safeguard personal information and to use it only to provide services to us.

| Provider | Function |

### 6.5 Legal and other disclosures

We may disclose personal information where required by law, where necessary to enforce our Terms of Use, or where necessary to investigate fraud or protect the safety of any person. If Acorns Nest is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal information may transfer as part of that transaction. It will be used only for the same purposes, it will remain subject to this policy until you are notified otherwise, and we will let centres know.

### 6.6 Where information is processed

Personal information handled by Acorns Nest is currently stored and processed in the United States. We are moving our hosting and data storage to Canada, in the Canada Central region in Montreal (ca-central-1), by the end of 2026. We will update this policy when the move is complete.

While information is outside Canada, it is subject to the laws of the jurisdiction in which it is held and may be accessible to courts, law enforcement, and regulatory authorities in that jurisdiction. We apply contractual and technical protections comparable to those we apply domestically and remain accountable for personal information transferred to a service provider.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

The mobile app does not use cookies or advertising identifiers. Session information is held in your device's secure storage and cleared when you sign out.

The director dashboard uses strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to remember which centre you are viewing.

On this website, Google Analytics runs only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. Before you choose, and if you click Decline, we do not load Google Analytics and we do not set an analytics cookie.

If you accept, we use Google Analytics to see which pages of this website are read, how you arrived (including from a link we shared), and whether the walkthrough form was submitted. A submission is counted only. Your name, email address, centre name, and phone number are not sent to Google. Those details are emailed to us, as described in section 6.4.

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not send information about children, families, or centre accounts to Google Analytics. The mobile app and the director dashboard are not included.

Your choice is stored in a first-party cookie for 6 months so the banner does not appear on every visit. That cookie records only whether you accepted or declined. If you accept, Google sets its own analytics cookies, which can remain on your browser for up to 2 years. The analytics data Google holds for us is kept for 2 months.

You can change your choice at any time from Cookie settings in the footer. If you withdraw consent, we stop Google Analytics and delete Google's analytics cookies. This choice applies only to visitors of this website. It is separate from the consent your childcare centre obtains at enrolment.

We do not use advertising cookies on the Service or on this website.

## 8. How long we keep information

Your centre decides how long its records are kept while it uses the Service.

| Information | Retention period |
| --- | --- |
| Children's profiles, care records, and photographs | Kept while your centre uses the Service, until the centre deletes them or asks us to delete them |
| Parent accounts | Deleted once the account is no longer linked to an enrolled child |
| Staff accounts | Deleted once the individual is no longer associated with any centre |
| Timesheets and other centre records | Kept while your centre uses the Service, until the centre deletes them or asks us to delete them |
| Notification tokens | Deleted on sign-out, when notifications are disabled, or when a device stops accepting them |
| Website enquiries | 2 years |
| Website analytics | The cookie that stores your choice is kept for 6 months. Google Analytics cookies may remain for up to 2 years. The analytics data held by Google is kept for 2 months. |

Data export and deletion. A centre can request a data export of its records at any time. When a centre asks us to delete its data, or stops using the Service, we delete its data from our servers 60 days after the request. This gives the centre time to take a backup of its data first. Each centre is responsible for keeping any records it must retain under childcare licensing and employment rules.

## 9. How we protect information

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold. These include encryption of personal information in transit and at rest, hashed password storage, access controls that limit access by role, and logged, founder-only access as described in section 6.2. Photographs are stored privately and require director approval before becoming visible to families.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach is caused by us, Acorns Nest Inc. takes responsibility for it. We will notify affected centres promptly, give them the information they need, and work with them to notify affected individuals. Where a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, it will be reported to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by law.

## 10. Children's privacy

Acorns Nest is designed for use by adults. Children do not hold accounts and cannot sign in. Information about a child is entered by centre staff or by that child's parent or guardian.

Consent for a child's information to be recorded in Acorns Nest is obtained by the childcare centre from the parent or guardian as part of enrolment. A parent or guardian exercises the rights described in section 11 on the child's behalf.

If you believe information about a child has been recorded without appropriate consent, contact your centre's director or email admin@acornsnest.com. We will investigate with the centre and remove the information where consent was not obtained.

## 11. Your rights

Subject to the limits set out in applicable privacy legislation, you may:

- Access the personal information we hold about you or your child, and be informed of how it has been used and to whom it has been disclosed
- Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
- Withdraw consent, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. Withdrawing consent for a child's record will generally mean the centre can no longer use the Service for that child, and the centre may need to keep certain records to meet licensing requirements.
- Request deletion of information that is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected or for a legal obligation. To delete an account, see section 12.
- Challenge our compliance with this policy

To make a request, contact your centre's director or email admin@acornsnest.com. We will verify your identity and respond within 30 days. If we require additional time or if a fee applies, we will tell you in advance.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at [priv.gc.ca](https://www.priv.gc.ca) or 1-800-282-1376. If your centre operates in Alberta, British Columbia, or Quebec, the provincial privacy commissioner may have jurisdiction.

## 12. Account deletion

Accounts are created by your childcare centre rather than by self-registration, and the app therefore does not provide a self-service deletion option.

To delete your account, email admin@acornsnest.com from the address associated with the account, or ask your centre's director to submit the request. A request must include the account holder's name and the name of the centre. We will verify your identity and confirm completion within the period stated in section 11.

Account deletion removes your credentials, contact details, profile, and device notification tokens. Two exceptions apply:

- A child's care record belongs to the centre rather than to an individual account. Deleting a parent account removes that parent's access and personal details. The child's record stays with the centre and is kept or deleted as described in section 8. If you also want the child's record deleted, state this in your request and we will coordinate with your centre.
- Records we are required by law to keep are kept for the required period and then deleted.

For staff and directors, account deletion also ends access to centre records in the Service.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, we will revise the "Last updated" date, notify centre directors, and obtain fresh consent through your centre where required. Continued use of the Service after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
